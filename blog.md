# 🩸 Day 1 – Feature Drop

DnHub is alive – the first powerful features have been implemented.

---

## ✅ Implemented Features

### 🎯 Triggerbot
- **Keybind**: Freely assignable key
- **Toggle**: Can be turned on/off
- **Part**: Targets specific hit zones (e.g. head, chest)

### 🎯 Aimlock
- **Keybind**: Direct activation key
- **Toggle**: Enable or disable with a keypress
- **Part**: Custom target zone
- **Smoothness**: Natural locking with adjustable smoothness

### 👁 ESP
- **Toggle**: Enable or disable the ESP
- **ColorPicker**: Choose your own Color
